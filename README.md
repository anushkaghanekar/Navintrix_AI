# Navintrix AI

> **AI-Driven Adaptive Traffic Signal Optimization Platform**

Navintrix AI is an intelligent traffic management system that leverages **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**, **Computer Vision (CV)**, and **Reinforcement Learning (RL)** to optimize traffic signal timings in real time. The platform analyzes live CCTV feeds to detect vehicle density, predict congestion, and dynamically adjust traffic signals to reduce waiting time, improve traffic flow, prioritize emergency vehicles, and lower fuel consumption and carbon emissions.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🚦 Adaptive Traffic Signal Optimization
- 📹 Real-Time Vehicle Detection
- 🤖 AI-Based Traffic Congestion Prediction
- 🚑 Emergency Vehicle Priority
- 📊 Interactive Analytics Dashboard
- 🗺️ Live Traffic Monitoring & Congestion Heatmaps
- 📈 Traffic Analytics & Reports

## ⚙️ Workflow

1. Capture live traffic through CCTV cameras.
2. Detect and classify vehicles using YOLOv8.
3. Analyze vehicle density and queue length.
4. Predict traffic congestion using AI models.
5. Optimize signal timings with Reinforcement Learning.
6. Prioritize emergency vehicles automatically.
7. Display real-time traffic analytics on the dashboard.
